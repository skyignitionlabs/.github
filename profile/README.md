# Sky Ignition Labs

### White-Label Web, AI & Security Engineering Partner for Agencies

We help agencies scale their technical delivery without adding full-time engineering overhead. Operating fully NDA-ready across US, UK, and Australian timezones, we act as your behind-the-scenes engineering team—from rapid MVP builds to legacy migrations and codebase security hardening.

---

### Core Capabilities

- **High-Converting Web & Custom Software:** Fast, modern websites, SaaS platforms, client portals, and mobile frontends built with Next.js, React, and TypeScript.
- **AI Chat, Voice & CRM Automation:** Custom AI triage agents, conversational booking engines, and deep GoHighLevel, Airtable, and Make.com workflows.
- **Legacy Migrations & Performance:** Zero-downtime migrations from slow legacy builders to modern, SEO-optimized architectures.
- **Security Audits & "Vibe Code" Cleanup:** Production hardening, authentication/RLS fixes, and refactoring fragile AI-generated codebases before client launch.
